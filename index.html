Weighted ranking settings

Total = five indicators × weights, normalised to 100 by the weight sum. The score is a reading priority, not fact-checking or an investment rating.

Lawsuits, credit stress, final closes and major deals rank first; law-firm solicitations and generic stock commentary are down-weighted. Reuters / Bloomberg / FT / WSJ are preset first, trade press second, press releases separately; the source is matched by its public name.

Institution relevance scores whether the headline names the firm; strategy relevance combines keyword hits with the semantic (HyDE) match; freshness is bucketed at 1 / 7 / 30 / 90 days from retrieval. Components are fixed at retrieval time; only the weighting is recomputed here.

All weights are zero, so every article scores 0.

Searches litigation, settlement and court-related news. A headline mention does not mean the institution is a defendant; no result does not mean no litigation exists. Verify cases and progress against the source and court records.
